BrandSync contrast overview

Fabri POS prototype, BrandSync mode, light and dark. Checked against WCAG 2.0 success criterion 1.4.3 (AA: 4.5:1 text, 3:1 large text) and 1.4.6 (AAA: 7:1). Ratios are computed from the token hex values. Non-text contrast (3:1 for control borders and focus rings) is a WCAG 2.1 criterion (1.4.11), so it is shown as information only.
